Mukti Guest House I

Sunderbans, India

Sustainable Bamboo Design

Mukti Guest House showcases a masterful application of sustainable bamboo design, blending ecological sensibility smoothly with premium hospitality facilities. Located in the unique climate of the Sunderbans, the structural system maximizes local building practices while setting a new baseline for high-performance mainstream green architecture.

The layout uses light structural grids, elevated platforms to accommodate geographical shifts, and pristine artisanal textures tailored to modern comfort requirements.

Project Location Sunderbans, West Bengal, India
Typology Hospitality, Eco-Tourism Guest House
Primary Materials Bespoke Engineered Bamboo, Local Thatch, Stone
Design Studio QX Design
