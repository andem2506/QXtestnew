About Us

Questions? and eXperiences!

Why? What? How? Wow!

Our approach emphasizes asking the right questions before conforming to quick solutions, ultimately leading to designs that provoke thought and inspire.The studio is committed to exploring alternative technologies through intensive site participation and grassroots innovation. By working closely with local contexts, QX Design develops sustainable and experimental solutions tailored to the needs of varied environments.

Our Team

We maintain a deep, collaborative relationship with our artisans, celebrating their craftsmanship as the foundation of our designs. By blending traditional techniques with contemporary ideas, we create spaces that honor cultural heritage. Engaging local craftsmen ensures authenticity and cultural resonance, reflecting the synergy of design and craftsmanship in every project.
