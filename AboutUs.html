About Us

QX Design

Questions? and eXperiences!

Udit Mittal is the principal architect and co-founder of QX Design Studio and Kirti Jalan Design Studio. An alumnus of SPA Delhi, Udit is passionate about exploring material-sensitive construction methods, focusing on sustainable and socially-engaged design solutions. His work often involves innovative use of bamboo, ferrocement, mud-lime, and debris walls, with a strong emphasis on community participation, particularly in rural contexts. Apart from architecture, Udit is interested in theatre, painting, and playing guitar.

Our Artisans

We maintain a deep and collaborative relationship with our artisans, celebrating their craftsmanship as the cornerstone of our designs. Together, we blend traditional techniques with contemporary ideas to create spaces that honor cultural heritage. We work hand-in-hand, respecting their traditional knowledge and integrating it seamlessly into our designs. By engaging local craftsmen, we ensure authenticity and cultural resonance in every project. Together, we celebrate the synergy of design and craftsmanship, creating spaces that reflect heritage and ingenuity. This partnership ensures every creation is both meaningful and unique.
